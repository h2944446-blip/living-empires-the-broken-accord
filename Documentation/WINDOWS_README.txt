LIVING EMPIRES: THE BROKEN ACCORD - COMMUNITY EDITION

1. Extract the entire ZIP to a folder you can write to.
2. Keep the executable, Data folder, runtime files, and license notices together.
3. Run LivingEmpiresCommunity.exe, then choose a chapter from the main menu.

First Winter Delivery: grow a settlement and complete its founding charter.
The Toll War: defend a prepared town with a small militia in a finite scenario.

Right-drag or WASD/arrow keys pans; mouse wheel zooms; middle-drag orbits/tilts.
Tab switches Settlement/Army mode. Army mode is available in The Toll War.
In Army mode, click or drag a box to select squads; Shift extends selection.
Short right-click moves or attacks. Army buttons provide explicit orders.
Space pauses; Escape cancels an action or opens the pause menu.
F5 saves the active chapter; F9 loads it. Save before changing chapters.
Use Settings to choose the graphics profile and adjust audio options.

Chapter saves are separate, under Unity's Application.persistentDataPath:
%USERPROFILE%\AppData\LocalLow\Living Empires\Living Empires Community Edition
Council scenes use text. This prototype has no full campaign or multiplayer.
Squads share health and recover from wounds; conquest and siege warfare are absent.
