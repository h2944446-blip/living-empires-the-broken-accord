# Roadmap

This is a direction for discussion, not a schedule or a promise that every feature will ship. Work is proposed through issues and reviewed in pull requests. The immediate priority is a reliable, understandable two-chapter game that new contributors can open and improve.

## Current foundation

- **First Winter Delivery:** worker assignments, production chains, town inventories and money, traveling trade, contracts, crossings, winter pressure, and recovery.
- **The Toll War:** a separate prepared settlement, four-person spearmen and archer squads, a six-squad cap, recruitment and upkeep, orders, barracks and equipment upgrades, three finite raids, camp victory, and wounded-unit recovery.
- Separate chapter saves, settlement and army controls, camera gestures, a minimap, a stylized valley, licensed imported scenery, and original procedural workers and soldiers.
- Text council presentation in the public edition, with no bundled external-service voice recordings or portraits.

## Near-term work

1. Make clean-clone setup and build instructions reproducible, and keep public validation tied to a specific revision.
2. Improve control discoverability, keyboard access, visual state cues, and readability at common desktop resolutions.
3. Strengthen focused checks around saves, crossing routes, recruitment, orders, and chapter switching when bugs are found.
4. Tune the two existing scenarios using recorded playtest observations, with explicit starting conditions and success criteria.
5. Make asset attribution, contributor credit, and build distributions easy to maintain.

The [starter issue candidates](Documentation/STARTER_ISSUES.md) break several of these goals into small reviewable changes.

## Possible later milestones

Queued waypoints and control groups could deepen squad control. More varied encounters, clearer campaign progression, additional original art, and richer settlement decisions could expand the two chapters. Each needs a scoped proposal and evidence that it improves the playable game.

Territorial conquest, siege warfare, cavalry, diplomacy treaties, a research tree, fog of war, multiplayer, bespoke heroes, and a full civilization campaign remain larger design possibilities. They are not implemented features or current delivery commitments.

## How priorities change

Reproducible defects, accessibility barriers, save integrity, and license or attribution errors take priority over expanding scope. A proposal should explain the player benefit, a small first implementation, and how reviewers can evaluate it. The project may narrow or reorder this roadmap as contributors learn from the game.
